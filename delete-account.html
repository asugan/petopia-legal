Petopia - Local Data and Subscription Help

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

The current Petopia app does not let users create Petopia accounts. Pet profiles and care records are stored locally on the device, so there is no Petopia account or Petopia-hosted pet profile to delete.

Clear data stored by the app

In Petopia, open Settings > Data & Privacy > Clear Local Data to reset the main app database. Some app preferences or unfinished setup information stored separately may remain after this action. Device backups may also preserve app data according to your device settings.

Removing Petopia may remove local app data from the device. Data that was included in an operating-system backup may be restored if that backup is restored.

Subscriptions and service-provider data

Petopia subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel a subscription in the relevant store account. Deleting local app data or uninstalling Petopia does not cancel a subscription. Purchase records may be retained by the store or RevenueCat under their policies and legal obligations.
