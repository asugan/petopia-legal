ColdLog is a tracking and informational tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a safety assessment. Cold-water immersion and breathwork can cause serious harm. ColdLog does not assess your health, monitor your body or water continuously, supervise a session, or contact emergency services.
