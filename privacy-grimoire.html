Privacy Policy - Grimoire: Digital Book of Shadows

Last Updated: November 14, 2026
Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Çağatay Erkin Eren ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Grimoire: Digital Book of Shadows (the "App"). The App is designed to keep your journal and other personal practice records on your device. Some App features do send limited information to the service providers described below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require an account and does not sync your personal records to a company-operated cloud account. Its local database and app storage may contain:

  • Display name, selected zodiac sign, language, theme, and app preferences.
  • Journal entry titles, text, moods, and dates.
  • Tarot spread details, selected cards, reading dates, and reflection notes.
  • Completed rituals, favorites, moon-phase details, notes, moods, and practice activity.
  • Birth date, optional birth time, birth place, and the latitude and longitude of that place, if you choose to enter birth details.
  • Rituals you create, including intentions, steps, materials, notes, and any photos you attach.
  • Materials you create, including names, types, notes, and any photos you attach, and your inventory of owned materials.
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly horoscope responses cached on your device without an automatic 24-hour expiry.

Birth-place search uses a city list bundled with the App, not a remote search service. Birth charts are calculated on your device. The App does not send your birth details, chart, journal text, or personal ritual and material content to our horoscope server or to an AI service. Tarot interpretations and birth chart reports use texts included with the App. The separate analytics events described below may include a selected zodiac sign.

Attached photos are stored as local files in the App's storage. The App uses the system photo picker to select a photo, or requests camera permission if you choose to take one.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your backup settings and platform. If you uninstall the App, local records may be removed from the device; copies in a device backup may remain.

3. Information Sent to Service Providers

Our horoscope service

The App requests pre-generated daily, weekly, and monthly horoscopes from our backend at grimoire.dekadans.net over HTTPS. Each request specifies the horoscope period, its start date, and the App language. The response contains all 12 zodiac signs, and the App selects your sign's text on your device. Your selected zodiac sign and birth details are not included in this request. The App no longer uses Free Horoscope API.

Our server and the hosting or network infrastructure handling the request process ordinary connection information, including your IP address and request headers, to deliver the response. Our public backend uses Cloudflare as a reverse proxy. Cloudflare processes connection and request information to deliver and protect the service, and may retain traffic and security data under its privacy policy and the applicable service configuration. Infrastructure access or security logs, if enabled, may include your IP address, request path, time, and response status. See Section 7 for retention information.

Horoscopes are generated in advance using AI from non-personal astronomical information. A request from the App reads existing content; it does not trigger AI generation or forward your request or IP address to the AI service. Downloaded horoscope text is cached locally by sign, period, start date, and language, and older cached text may be shown when newer content cannot be loaded.

Product analytics

In builds configured with an Aptabase app key, the App sends usage events to Aptabase. Events describe feature use and app interactions, and may include counts or simple status fields. When you change your zodiac setting, the selected sign is included in that event. The event payloads we create do not include the text of your journal entries or free-form tarot and ritual reflections. The SDK may attach an anonymous session identifier and technical context such as app version, platform, operating system, or locale. Aptabase describes its analytics as anonymous and says it does not use persistent device identifiers. Aptabase derives an approximate country and region from the IP address of each request; the IP address itself is not stored.

Crash and performance diagnostics

If a build is configured with a Sentry DSN, the App may send crash reports, error details, and sampled performance diagnostics to Sentry. These reports may include technical information needed to diagnose the problem, such as app version, operating system, device model, and stack traces. Sentry may also attach a random installation identifier generated by the SDK. This identifier resets when the App is reinstalled and is not an advertising identifier. Sentry is not configured in builds without a DSN.

Purchases and Apple Ads attribution

In builds configured with RevenueCat, the App initializes its SDK at startup to check premium access, even if you have not made a purchase. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and, where available, purchase and entitlement information, such as transaction or receipt information, product identifiers, and subscription status. It also supports buying and restoring purchases. Apple or Google processes payment details through its store; we do not receive your full payment card number.

On supported iOS versions, RevenueCat initialization also enables Apple's AdServices attribution token collection. RevenueCat can send this token to Apple to obtain attribution information, where available, about whether an installation came from an Apple Ads campaign. This helps measure campaign effectiveness and can occur without a purchase. An AdServices attribution token is not the IDFA advertising identifier. We do not send journal text, birth details, or personal ritual and material content for this purpose. The App does not provide an in-app switch to disable this token collection.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Local notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules them on your device. These are local notifications; the App does not use a server to send them.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide the App's journal, tarot, birth chart, ritual, materials, inventory, favorites, horoscope, and reminder features.
  • Understand feature use and improve the App through configured analytics.
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues when Sentry is configured.
  • Process purchases, restore purchases, and provide premium access.
  • Measure Apple Ads campaign effectiveness through the attribution described above.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot our horoscope service.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not use journal or reflection text, birth details, or personal ritual and material content for advertising. The App does not display third-party advertisements. Apple Ads installation attribution is described separately above.

5. Your Choices, Export, and Deletion

  • You can export available app records in formats such as JSON, text, or PDF using the App's export feature. JSON backups include personal rituals, materials, inventory, and attached photos; birth details and app settings are not included. If you share or save an export, the recipient app or storage provider handles that copy under its own terms. Deleting data in the App does not delete copies you have exported or shared.
  • You can delete individual journal entries, personal rituals, and personal materials using the in-app controls, and clear your saved birth details from the birth data screen. Photos no longer referenced by any ritual or material are cleaned up when the App next starts. The App does not currently create an online account, so there is no account to delete. There is no in-app control to disable Aptabase analytics in builds where analytics is configured.
  • Local data can be removed by uninstalling the App, subject to copies that may remain in operating-system backups. Purchase records are handled by the relevant app store and RevenueCat under their policies.

You may contact us to exercise privacy rights available under the laws that apply to you. Because Aptabase events are designed not to identify a specific user, we may not be able to locate or delete an individual analytics event.

6. Sharing and International Processing

We share information with the providers listed in this Policy only for the features they support, and when required by law or to protect the App and its users. Those providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply their own privacy terms. Please review their current policies using the links above.

7. Security and Retention

Personal practice records remain in the App's local database until you delete them, remove the App, or your device otherwise removes them. The security of local records also depends on your device passcode, operating system, and backup settings. Attached photos remain in local app storage; unused photo files are cleaned up at the next App startup. Local horoscope cache entries do not have a fixed expiry or an automatic 24-hour deletion rule and may remain until the App's local data is removed.

The horoscope backend application does not implement routine IP-address access logging. Its operational logs cover service startup, generation activity, and errors, and are written to the container's standard error output. The current deployment stores container output using Docker's JSON-file logging driver without automatic log rotation or a time-based expiry. These operational logs therefore have no fixed maximum retention period and can remain until the container is removed or the logs are separately deleted. Restarting the container does not clear them.

Cloudflare's traffic and security data are separate from these Docker operational logs. Their retention depends on the Cloudflare services and settings in use and Cloudflare's privacy policy; the absence of routine IP-address access logging in our backend does not mean that Cloudflare retains no connection data. We do not specify a single fixed deletion period for Cloudflare data. You may contact us about connection data, operational logs, and applicable access or deletion rights using the details below.

Information sent to third-party providers, including purchase and Apple Ads attribution information, is retained according to their policies and service settings. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a provider-backed feature, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or our data practices change. We will revise the date above and provide an in-app notice when required.
