Privacy Policy - Grimoire: Digital Book of Shadows

Last Updated: September 23, 2026
Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Çağatay Erkin Eren ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Grimoire: Digital Book of Shadows (the "App"). The App is designed to keep your journal and other personal practice records on your device. Some App features do send limited information to the service providers described below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require an account and does not sync your personal records to a company-operated cloud account. Its local database may contain:

  • Display name, selected zodiac sign, language, theme, and app preferences.
  • Journal entry titles, text, moods, and dates.
  • Tarot spread details, selected cards, reading dates, and reflection notes.
  • Completed rituals, favorites, moon-phase details, notes, moods, and practice activity.
  • Horoscope responses cached on your device for up to 24 hours.

These records are stored locally and are not sent to our own account servers. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your backup settings and platform. If you uninstall the App, local records may be removed from the device; copies in a device backup may remain.

3. Information Sent to Service Providers

Daily horoscope service

When you request a horoscope, the App sends the selected zodiac sign to Free Horoscope API as part of the request. The provider may also receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request headers. The horoscope response is cached locally for up to 24 hours.

Product analytics

In builds configured with an Aptabase app key, the App sends usage events to Aptabase. Events describe feature use and app interactions, and may include counts or simple status fields. When you change your zodiac setting, the selected sign is included in that event. The event payloads we create do not include the text of your journal entries or free-form tarot and ritual reflections. The SDK may attach an anonymous session identifier and technical context such as app version, platform, operating system, or locale. Aptabase describes its analytics as anonymous and says it does not use persistent device identifiers.

Crash and performance diagnostics

If a build is configured with a Sentry DSN, the App may send crash reports, error details, and sampled performance diagnostics to Sentry. These reports may include technical information needed to diagnose the problem, such as app version, operating system, and stack traces. Sentry is not configured in builds without a DSN.

Purchases

If you buy or restore a subscription or one-time purchase, RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement information to verify access. This can include transaction or receipt information, product and subscription status, and an anonymous app-user identifier. Apple or Google processes payment details through its store; we do not receive your full payment card number. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Local notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules them on your device. These are local notifications; the App does not use a server to send them.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide the App's journal, tarot, ritual, favorites, horoscope, and reminder features.
  • Understand feature use and improve the App through configured analytics.
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues when Sentry is configured.
  • Process purchases, restore purchases, and provide premium access.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not use journal or reflection text for advertising. The App does not include an advertising feature or cross-app advertising tracker.

5. Your Choices, Export, and Deletion

  • You can export available app records in formats such as JSON, text, or PDF using the App's export feature. If you share or save an export, the recipient app or storage provider handles that copy under its own terms.
  • You can delete individual journal entries using the in-app controls. The App does not currently create an online account, so there is no account to delete. There is no in-app control to disable Aptabase analytics in builds where analytics is configured.
  • Local data can be removed by uninstalling the App, subject to copies that may remain in operating-system backups. Purchase records are handled by the relevant app store and RevenueCat under their policies.

You may contact us to exercise privacy rights available under the laws that apply to you. Because Aptabase events are designed not to identify a specific user, we may not be able to locate or delete an individual analytics event.

6. Sharing and International Processing

We share information with the providers listed in this Policy only for the features they support, and when required by law or to protect the App and its users. Those providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply their own privacy terms. Please review their current policies using the links above.

7. Security and Retention

Personal practice records remain in the App's local database until you delete them, remove the App, or your device otherwise removes them. The security of local records also depends on your device passcode, operating system, and backup settings. Information sent to third-party providers is retained according to their policies and service settings. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a provider-backed feature, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or our data practices change. We will revise the date above and provide an in-app notice when required.
