Privacy Policy - Cloud Identifier

Last Updated: November 22, 2026
Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Çağatay Erkin Eren ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Cloud Identifier (the "App"). The App identifies clouds in photos of the sky. Cloud identification uses an AI model provided by OpenAI, a third-party service: when you scan a photo, a resized copy of that photo is sent to OpenAI. Section 3 explains this in detail. Your collection of saved sightings stays on your device.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require an account and does not sync your data to a company-operated cloud account. Its local database and app storage may contain:

  • Sightings you save: the resized sky photo, the date and time it was saved, and the identified cloud types with their confidence and the AI's note about the sky.
  • A temporary copy of each scanned photo in the App's cache, which the operating system may clear.
  • Whether you have completed onboarding and whether you have allowed AI cloud identification.
  • A cached copy of your subscription status (whether it is active and when it expires), so the App can open without a connection.

To take a photo, the App asks for camera permission. To choose an existing photo, the App uses the system photo picker and receives only the photo you select. The App does not request your location.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your backup settings and platform. If you uninstall the App, local data may be removed from the device; copies in a device backup may remain.

3. Cloud Identification with AI (OpenAI)

Your permission

Before your first scan, the App explains that cloud identification uses an AI model from OpenAI and asks for your permission. If you choose "Not now," no photo is sent, and the App asks again the next time you start a scan. The App cannot identify clouds without sending the photo.

What is sent

When you scan a photo, the App first resizes it on your device (at most 1280 pixels on the longest side) and saves it as a new JPEG image. This new image does not carry over the original photo's metadata, such as the location where it was taken. The App then sends the resized photo over HTTPS to our scan server (Section 4), which forwards it to an AI model provided by OpenAI together with fixed instructions and the list of cloud types the App knows. OpenAI returns the cloud types it identifies, a confidence level, and a short note about the sky, which the App shows to you.

OpenAI receives the photo and our instructions only. We do not send OpenAI your name, an account, your purchase or subscription identifier, your IP address, or your location. However, the photo itself may show whatever is in the frame, such as buildings, landscapes, or people. We recommend photographing only the sky.

OpenAI processes the photo to provide the identification and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. We use the AI results only to show them to you; we do not use your photos for advertising.

4. Our Scan Server

Scans go through a server we operate, which checks your subscription, applies usage limits, and forwards the photo to OpenAI. The server is reached through Cloudflare, which processes connection and request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the service and may retain traffic and security data under its privacy policy and the applicable service configuration.

For each scan, our server receives:

  • The resized photo. It is held in memory only while the request is processed and is not saved to disk or written to logs.
  • An anonymous app-user identifier created by RevenueCat. The server sends this identifier to RevenueCat to check that you have an active subscription, and keeps the answer in memory for up to 5 minutes. To apply the daily scan limit, the server stores this identifier with the number of scans made on the current UTC day. Rows from earlier days are deleted when the next scan is counted; the server does not keep a scan history per user. The server also stores a total scan count per day for the whole service, which contains no user information.
  • Your IP address and request headers. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address in memory over one-minute windows. These counts are not written to disk or logs and are lost when the server restarts.

The server writes one log line per scan with technical details: the outcome, how long it took, the number of AI tokens used, whether the photo showed the sky, and how many clouds were found. These log lines do not include the photo, the app-user identifier, or your IP address. Error logs record technical failures without request content. Logs are rotated automatically: at most three files of 10 MB each are kept, and the oldest is deleted when a new one starts.

5. Other Service Providers

Product analytics

In builds configured with an Aptabase app key, the App sends usage events to Aptabase. Events describe app use, such as app opens, completing onboarding (with your optional answer to "Why do you look at the sky?"), allowing AI identification, starting a scan (camera or photo library), the scan outcome and number of clouds found or the type of error, saving or deleting a sighting, creating a share card, and paywall and purchase steps (such as the selected billing period). Events do not include your photos. The SDK may attach an anonymous session identifier and technical context such as app version, platform, operating system, or locale. Aptabase describes its analytics as anonymous and says it does not use persistent device identifiers. Aptabase derives an approximate country and region from the IP address of each request; the IP address itself is not stored.

Crash and performance diagnostics

If a build is configured with a Sentry DSN, the App may send crash reports, error details, and sampled performance diagnostics to Sentry. These reports may include technical information needed to diagnose the problem, such as app version, operating system, device model, screen names, and stack traces. They do not include your photos. Sentry may also attach a random installation identifier generated by the SDK. This identifier resets when the App is reinstalled and is not an advertising identifier. Sentry is not configured in builds without a DSN.

Purchases and Apple Ads attribution

The App uses RevenueCat to sell and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat's SDK starts when the App opens to check your access, even if you have not made a purchase. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and, where available, purchase and entitlement information, such as transaction or receipt information, product identifiers, and subscription status. It also supports buying and restoring purchases. Apple or Google processes payment details through its store; we do not receive your full payment card number.

On supported iOS versions, RevenueCat initialization also enables Apple's AdServices attribution token collection. RevenueCat can send this token to Apple to obtain attribution information, where available, about whether an installation came from an Apple Ads campaign. This helps measure campaign effectiveness and can occur without a purchase. An AdServices attribution token is not the IDFA advertising identifier. The App does not provide an in-app switch to disable this token collection.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Local notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the App schedules reminders about your trial on your device. These are local notifications; the App does not use a server to send them.

Sharing

If you tap "Share" on a result, the App creates a share image on your device and opens the system share sheet. The app or service you choose handles the shared image under its own terms.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Identify clouds in your photos and show the results.
  • Provide the collection, atlas, share card, and trial reminder features.
  • Check your subscription and apply fair-use limits on scans.
  • Process purchases, restore purchases, and provide premium access.
  • Understand feature use and improve the App through configured analytics.
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues when Sentry is configured.
  • Measure Apple Ads campaign effectiveness through the attribution described above.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot our scan server.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not sell your data. The App does not display third-party advertisements, and we do not use your photos for advertising.

7. Your Choices and Deletion

  • You can delete any saved sighting and its photo in the App, from the result screen or by swiping the sighting in your collection.
  • If you do not want photos sent to OpenAI, choose "Not now" when asked, or do not start a scan. Your answer is stored on your device; removing the App's data or reinstalling the App resets it. There is currently no in-app switch to withdraw permission after you allow it.
  • Local data can be removed by uninstalling the App, subject to copies that may remain in operating-system backups. Purchase records are handled by the relevant app store and RevenueCat under their policies.
  • There is no in-app control to disable Aptabase analytics in builds where analytics is configured.

The App does not create an online account, so there is no account to delete. You may contact us to exercise privacy rights available under the laws that apply to you. Because Aptabase events are designed not to identify a specific user, we may not be able to locate or delete an individual analytics event.

8. Sharing and International Processing

We share information with the providers listed in this Policy only for the features they support, and when required by law or to protect the App and its users. Those providers, including OpenAI, may process information in countries other than your own and apply their own privacy terms. Please review their current policies using the links above.

9. Security and Retention

Saved sightings remain on your device until you delete them, remove the App, or your device otherwise removes them. The security of local data also depends on your device passcode, operating system, and backup settings.

On our scan server, photos are not stored; daily scan counts and logs are kept as described in Section 4. Information sent to third-party providers, including OpenAI, Cloudflare, RevenueCat, Aptabase, Sentry, and Apple or Google, is retained according to their policies and service settings. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us so we can review the request.
